Some engines are transportation. A handful are instruments. Everything I write about cars starts from that difference — and from wondering how long the industry will keep building the second kind.

Not nostalgia — attention. A clutch pedal asks something of you that a paddle shifter never will. Notes on the last cars still asking.

Watching the industry turn
