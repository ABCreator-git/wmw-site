Ignition

Turn the key and something old wakes up —
not the engine first, but the wanting.
Metal remembers every road
the way a hand remembers a name
it hasn't said in years.

— A.B.

Night Drive, Radio Low

Somewhere between two stations
is where I keep my heart:
static soft enough to sleep in,
a signal I can half make out,
a highway that forgives the dark
for taking its own time.

— A.B.

Odometer

Every number here is a small grief —
a mile I will not drive again.
And still I press on,
because the road behind
was never the point.

— A.B.
